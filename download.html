Epic Fury Studio is for people invited to a team. Use the name and setup code your team gave you.

Getting started

  1. DownloadPress Download above, with your name and setup code when it asks for them. The file is about 800 MB.
  2. InstallOpen the downloaded file and drag Epic Fury Studio into your Applications folder.
  3. OpenOpen it from Applications. The first time, your Mac says it can’t check the app: press Done. Then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, press Open Anyway beside Epic Fury Studio, and type your Mac’s password. You only do this once.
  4. Log inPress First time? Use your setup code, type your name and code, and choose a password. Your studio’s artists and settings come with you.
  5. Set up this MacThe app walks you through Google Drive: install it, allow access, and sign in with the Google account your studio shared its folders with.

Updates

New versions arrive inside the app and install when you restart it.

Connecting your accounts

Connect your accounts inside the app, on each platform’s own sign-in page. The app never sees those passwords. More in the Privacy Policy.
