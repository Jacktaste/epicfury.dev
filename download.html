Getting started

  1. DownloadPress the button above. It is one file, and it is the whole app.
  2. InstallOpen the downloaded file and drag Epic Fury Studio into your Applications folder.
  3. OpenOpen it from Applications like any other app. The first time, macOS checks it before it starts.
  4. Log inUse your setup code once, or start a studio of your own. Your studio’s artists and settings arrive with you.

What is inside

Everything it runs on travels in the one download: the video engine, the caption maker, and the models that line lyrics up with a song. None of it is fetched or built on your Mac after you install, so there is nothing to go wrong halfway.

Updates arrive inside the app. When a new version is ready, Epic Fury Studio offers it and installs it itself.

Connecting your accounts

Publishing and performance figures need you to connect your own accounts, from inside the app, on each platform’s own sign-in page. The app never sees those passwords. How that works, and what it can and cannot read, is set out on the home page and in the Privacy Policy.
