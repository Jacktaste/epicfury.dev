Clip Studio

A video editor that publishes where you already post.

Epic Fury Clip Studio is a desktop application for making short-form video. It cuts clips to music, burns in captions, and publishes the finished video straight to the social accounts you connect.

What it does

  • Cuts to the music. It finds where a clip sits inside a track and trims it to the beat.
  • Burns in captions. Lyrics and speech are timed against the audio, styled, and rendered into the video.
  • Publishes. Connect an account once, then send a finished video to it without leaving the app.
  • Reads back how it did. For accounts the user has connected, the app reads that account’s own public details and its own performance figures — views, watch time, subscriber counts — so the person who made a video can see whether it worked.

How connecting an account works

You sign in on the platform's own page — TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook — and approve the connection there. The app never sees your password. The access token it receives is stored on your own computer.

Nothing is posted unless a person chooses to post it. The app does not read your feed, your messages, or your browsing activity, and it does not post on a schedule you did not set.

Why it asks to read as well as publish

A person who makes a video wants to know how it did. So, for the accounts you connect, the app also asks for read-only access to that account's own details and its own performance figures.

On YouTube that means two things and no more:

  • See your YouTube account — the channel's name, its video list and its public counts, so a video the app published can be matched to the right channel and listed by name rather than by ID.
  • See your YouTube Analytics reports — views, watch time, traffic sources and audience figures for that channel's own videos, so the team can see which of their videos worked.

These are read-only. They cannot change, upload or delete anything. A channel can be connected for analytics alone, with no ability to publish to it at all — that is a choice made when the account is connected, and the app then never offers that channel as somewhere to post.

The figures are shown to the team member who asked for them and stored on their own computer. They are not sent to us, sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.

You can disconnect an account from inside the app at any time, or revoke access from the platform's own settings — for Google accounts, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Availability

Epic Fury Clip Studio is a private tool built for the GAWNE music team. It is not currently offered to the public and there is no sign-up.
