Clip Studio

A video editor that publishes where you already post.

Epic Fury Clip Studio is a desktop application for making short-form video. It cuts clips to music, burns in captions, and publishes the finished video straight to the social accounts you connect.

What it does

  • Cuts to the music. It finds where a clip sits inside a track and trims it to the beat.
  • Burns in captions. Lyrics and speech are timed against the audio, styled, and rendered into the video.
  • Publishes. Connect an account once, then send a finished video to it without leaving the app.

How connecting an account works

You sign in on the platform's own page — TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook — and approve the connection there. The app never sees your password. The access token it receives is stored on your own computer and used only to publish the videos you tell it to publish.

Nothing is posted unless a person chooses to post it. The app does not read your feed, your followers, or your messages, and it does not post on a schedule you did not set.

You can disconnect an account from inside the app at any time, or revoke access from the platform's own settings.

Availability

Epic Fury Clip Studio is a private tool built for the GAWNE music team. It is not currently offered to the public and there is no sign-up.
